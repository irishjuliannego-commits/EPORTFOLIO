Introduction

Professional Profile

I am a Licensed Geodetic Engineer and Civil Service Passer with a Bachelor of Science in Geodetic Engineering degree from Caraga State University – Main Campus in Butuan City.

My practical background encompasses hands-on fieldwork and technical office work gained through my student internship with BPA Abatayo Land Surveying Services. Through this experience, I developed solid practical proficiency in executing relocation, subdivision, and topographic surveys, property boundary verification involving titled lands, and conducting land records research and document verification at the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR).

Complementing my technical competencies, I have served in key executive leadership roles as President and Treasurer of the Young Geodetic Engineering Students Society (YGESS) and as a Board of Director of the Mindanao Association of Geodetic Engineering Students (MAGES), demonstrating proven accountability, teamwork, and organizational dedication.
